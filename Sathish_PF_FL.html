About Me

Where Pedagogy
Meets Data & AI

I work across three closely linked disciplines: end-to-end learning solution design — needs analysis, storyboarding, SCORM-compliant modules — data analysis — cleaning, querying, and visualizing the LMS/business data that shows whether those solutions succeed — and AI engineering — building the prompt pipelines, LLM API integrations, and automation tools that make both faster and smarter. My work spans corporate compliance training, finance process education, university-level curriculum, and onboarding experiences across global organizations.

What sets my approach apart is a hands-on command of AI tooling across all three — Synthesia for video content generation, Copilot and Power BI Copilot for faster analysis, and direct integration with LLM APIs and prompt engineering — enabling faster production and sharper insight without sacrificing quality.

Approach

Design
Methodology

Every project draws on proven frameworks across AI engineering, data analysis, and eLearning design — ensuring strategic alignment from analysis through evaluation.

ADDIE
Analysis, Design, Development, Implementation, Evaluation
The gold-standard framework guiding systematic course development from needs analysis through iterative evaluation and continuous improvement.
SAM
Successive Approximation Model
An agile, iterative approach enabling rapid prototyping and stakeholder feedback loops to accelerate time-to-delivery without sacrificing quality.
BT
Bloom's Taxonomy
Applied to craft measurable learning objectives across cognitive levels — from foundational recall through synthesis — ensuring outcomes are observable and achievable.
Agile/Scrum
Iterative Analytics Delivery
Sprint-based approach to data requests and dashboard development, enabling rapid iteration, continuous stakeholder feedback, and prioritized delivery of insights.
Prompt Design
Iterative Prompt Engineering & API Integration
Design, test, and refine prompts across multiple LLM providers, then wire the best-performing pipeline into a working API-based solution for content or workflow tasks.
